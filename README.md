## 👋 About Me

I'm **Adefemi Adelugba**, an **Anatomy graduate**, Environmental Health professional, and aspiring **Public Health/Data Analyst** currently pursuing an **MSc in Public Health**.

My professional experience in the **Environmental Health Unit of the Lagos State Ministry of the Environment and Water Resources** has exposed me to environmental health operations, field data collection, reporting, data management, administrative processes, and public health interventions.

I'm now combining my background in **Anatomy and Public Health** with **Data Science and Analytics** to develop practical, data-driven solutions to health and environmental problems.

I am particularly interested in the intersection of:

**🏥 Public Health + 🌍 Environmental Health + 📊 Data Science + 🗺️ Spatial Analysis**
